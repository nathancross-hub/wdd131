Our Mission

What is bonzai?

At bonzai Furniture, we find furniture that is no longer wanted because it is damaged, broken, or simply needs a fresh start. We repair and restore each piece so it can become useful again.

Our goal is to make quality furniture more affordable while reducing waste. Instead of sending a good piece of furniture to the landfill, we give it another chance to become part of someone’s home.
